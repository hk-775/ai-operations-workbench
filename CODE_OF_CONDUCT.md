# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Critique ideas and code rather than
people, welcome questions, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, doxxing, and unwanted contact are
not acceptable. Never share someone else's credentials, recordings, or personal
information.

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation
when necessary to keep the project constructive. Report conduct concerns to
the maintainer through GitHub's private reporting tools; avoid posting private
details in public issues.
