USAGE OVERVIEW

Know what your tasks cost.

See how much the captured model calls cost, how many task runs they belong to, and where information is missing.

How to read these costs

What is included in the estimate?

Token counts reported by the provider are multiplied by the saved model prices. Calls with missing usage or unsupported prices remain unknown. A known subtotal can therefore be lower than the full cost.

Hardware and electricity are unmeasured. Provider invoices have not been reconciled. Calls captured only at the final response may omit retries and failed attempts. Synthetic test data is excluded unless you select it above.
